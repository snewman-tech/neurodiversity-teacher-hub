What ADHD can affect at school

ADHD can influence attention regulation, inhibition, working memory, time awareness, motivation, emotional regulation and executive function. Capacity can look inconsistent across tasks and days.

Starting

Use a tiny first action: “Open the document and write the heading.” Check back after initiation.

Working memory

Keep instructions visible. Avoid requiring a student to hold a long sequence in mind.

Time

Use visual timers, interim deadlines and transition warnings.

Organisation

Create one consistent place for deadlines, resources, homework and required materials.

Movement

Purposeful movement can support attention. Offer standing, stretch breaks or delivery jobs.

Feedback

Make feedback immediate, specific and private. Teach skills rather than relying on delayed consequences alone.

Remember: intense focus on an interesting task does not rule out ADHD. Attention regulation is not the same as simply having “too little attention”.
