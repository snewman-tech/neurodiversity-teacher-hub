Type what you are seeing: “won’t start”, “noise”, “group work”, “forgot homework”, “shutdown”, “change”.

Task initiation

Student cannot get started

  • Give one tiny first action.
  • Show an example of the finished product.
  • Reduce choices at the starting point.
  • Return in 2–3 minutes rather than repeating the whole instruction.
Sensory overload

Noise, light or crowding is consuming attention

  • Offer a quieter location or sensory tool.
  • Allow a neutral exit before distress peaks.
  • Reduce unnecessary environmental input.
  • Plan high-sensory events in advance.
Transitions

Change is causing distress or refusal

  • Give advance warning and a written/visual update.
  • State what is changing and what is staying the same.
  • Clarify the very next step.
  • Allow recovery after unusually demanding transitions.
Distress

Student is shutting down or escalating

  • Lower language and demands.
  • Create space and reduce the audience.
  • Use familiar regulation supports.
  • Save teaching, consequences and reflection for after recovery.
Organisation

Materials, homework or deadlines keep disappearing

  • Use one consistent digital/physical location.
  • Make deadlines visible and chunked.
  • Schedule pack-up rather than expecting it to happen automatically.
  • Teach the system explicitly and rehearse it.
Impulse control

Calling out or interrupting

  • Use a discreet participation cue.
  • Increase opportunities for active response.
  • Give movement and speaking roles.
  • Correct briefly and privately rather than repeatedly shaming publicly.
Group work

Group work is the hardest part of the lesson

  • Assign clear roles.
  • Make social rules explicit.
  • Offer pair or independent alternatives where appropriate.
  • Teach the task before adding the social demand.
Easy to miss

Quiet, compliant or high-achieving — but struggling

  • Check workload and effort, not just output.
  • Ask about exhaustion after school.
  • Notice perfectionism and avoidance of mistakes.
  • Offer support without requiring visible disruption first.
