Universal classroom supports

Instructions

Say less, show more

  • Number the steps.
  • Keep them visible.
  • Show an example.
  • Check privately.
Tasks

Reduce activation energy

  • Chunk long tasks.
  • Highlight the first step.
  • Use interim deadlines.
  • Make “done” visible.
Environment

Reduce irrelevant load

  • Offer quieter locations.
  • Reduce clutter where useful.
  • Allow appropriate movement.
  • Make routines predictable.
Transitions

Preview change

  • Give countdowns.
  • Explain unusual events.
  • Show what comes next.
  • Plan recovery.
Assessment

Assess the intended skill

  • Clarify criteria.
  • Reduce irrelevant barriers.
  • Support time planning.
  • Use accessible formats where permitted.
Regulation

Make breaks neutral

  • Offer planned breaks.
  • Teach how to request one.
  • Keep regulation tools available.
  • Return without shame.

Behaviour: change the question

Old question

“How do I make them stop?”

This can lead to removing behaviour without understanding what function, communication or regulation need sits underneath it.

Better question

“What is making this hard, and what skill or support is missing?”

This opens the door to environmental changes, explicit teaching, co-regulation and accessible expectations.
