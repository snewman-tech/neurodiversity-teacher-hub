Universal supports

Instructions

  • Say less, show more
  • Use numbered steps
  • Give examples of finished work
  • Check privately for understanding

Tasks

  • Chunk long work
  • Mark a clear starting point
  • Offer interim deadlines
  • Provide output choices where possible

Environment

  • Reduce unnecessary visual clutter
  • Offer quieter working locations
  • Make routines predictable
  • Allow appropriate movement

Transitions

  • Give 10/5/2 minute warnings
  • Preview unusual events
  • Clarify the next location and adult
  • Provide recovery after high-demand events

Regulation

  • Co-regulate before problem solving
  • Provide neutral break options
  • Teach body/sensory awareness
  • Do not remove regulation tools as punishment

Assessment

  • Clarify exactly what is being assessed
  • Reduce irrelevant barriers
  • Support planning and time awareness
  • Offer accessible formats where permitted
