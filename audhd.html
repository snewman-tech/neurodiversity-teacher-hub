When ADHD and autism overlap

Some learners are both autistic and ADHD. Their needs can appear contradictory, which is one reason rigid strategies often fail.

Routine + novelty

“I need structure, but I struggle to maintain it.”

Use stable anchors with flexible choices inside them: a predictable lesson shape, plus options for task order, seating or output.

Stimulation + overload

“I seek input, then suddenly have too much.”

Offer multiple regulation options and teach the learner how to notice when their needs change.

Connection + recovery

“I want people, but socialising is tiring.”

Make participation flexible and allow low-demand recovery without framing it as exclusion.

Interest + task initiation

“I care deeply, but still can’t start.”

Separate motivation from executive function. Provide a concrete first step, external structure and a clear endpoint.
