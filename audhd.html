AuDHD: when ADHD and autism overlap

Flexible structure can work better than rigid one-size-fits-all strategies when ADHD and autism overlap.

HomeFind SupportADHDAutismAuDHDMissed & MaskedStrategiesTeacher ToolkitResourcesNZ SupportStaff PLD

Needs can pull in different directions

A learner with both ADHD and autism may need structure yet struggle to maintain routines, seek stimulation yet become overloaded, or crave social connection yet need substantial recovery afterwards.

Routine + flexibility

Stable anchors, flexible choices

Keep the lesson shape predictable while allowing choice in task order, seating, method or output.

Input + overload

Regulation needs change

Offer more than one regulation option so the student can seek or reduce input as needed.

Interest + initiation

Caring does not guarantee starting

Deep interest may coexist with executive-function barriers. Make the first action concrete.

Social + recovery

Connection can still be tiring

Do not interpret recovery time as rejection or antisocial behaviour.
