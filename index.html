Start here: five supports that help tomorrow

Use these proactively. A learner does not need to disclose a diagnosis to benefit from clear, accessible teaching.

Make it visible

Show instructions

Pair spoken instructions with written steps, examples, visual schedules, and clear “finished” points.

Chunk it

Reduce cognitive load

Give one or two steps at a time, especially for long or unfamiliar tasks.

Regulation

Build in movement & sensory options

Offer movement, quiet spaces, flexible seating, and appropriate sensory tools without shame.

Predictability

Prepare for transitions

Give warnings, explain changes early, and make the next step clear.

Connection

Be curious about behaviour

Ask what is making the situation hard before assuming defiance or lack of effort.

Dignity

Support privately

Avoid public correction where possible, forced eye contact, and supports that unnecessarily single students out.
