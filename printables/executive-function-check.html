Executive-function barrier check

Use this when a capable learner is not consistently turning knowledge into action.

  • Starting: Do they know the first concrete action?
  • Working memory: Must they remember several steps?
  • Planning: Is the task too open-ended?
  • Time: Can they tell how long remains?
  • Switching: Is moving between tasks difficult?
  • Inhibition: Does acting before thinking create barriers?
  • Monitoring: Can they tell whether they are on track?
  • Emotion: Is distress blocking access to skills they usually have?
