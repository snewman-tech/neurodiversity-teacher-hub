Teacher quick sheet

A one-page reminder for accessible classroom support.

Before correcting behaviour, ask

  • Is the instruction clear and visible?
  • Does the learner know how to start?
  • Is sensory load too high?
  • Is working-memory demand hidden?
  • Is this overload, confusion, regulation or a missing skill?

Try tomorrow

  • Written steps
  • One tiny first action
  • Visual timer
  • Transition warning
  • Quiet or movement option
  • Private check-in
