Sensory environment check

Scan the environment before assuming the barrier is motivation or behaviour.

  • Sound: voices, scraping chairs, bells, machinery, music
  • Light: glare, flicker, brightness, projector
  • Visual load: clutter, movement, crowded walls
  • Touch: uniforms, seating, accidental contact, materials
  • Smell: food, cleaning products, perfume, science/art materials
  • Movement: crowded corridors, assemblies, queues
  • Temperature: heat, cold, airflow
