A practical school pathway

Teachers do not need to diagnose in order to reduce barriers. Support can begin while the school gathers information and works with the learner and whānau.

Notice patterns and barriers

Describe what is happening, when, where, and what conditions make it easier or harder.

Ask the learner

Find out what they experience and what they think would help.

Check existing information

Review current plans, previous strategies and known learning or health information available through appropriate school channels.

Work with whānau and learning support

Use your school’s SENCO/LSC or equivalent pathway and share observations without making diagnostic claims.

Trial adjustments

Use reasonable classroom supports and record whether access, participation or wellbeing improves.

Seek specialist support where needed

Follow school and Ministry pathways, including RTLB and other appropriate services.
