Where teachers can turn in Aotearoa

A diagnosis is not the only starting point for support. Teachers can document needs, trial accessible strategies, work with whānau and use school learning-support pathways.

In school

Learning Support Coordinator / SENCO

Use your school’s internal process to discuss patterns, classroom supports, whānau partnership and whether specialist input may be useful.

Specialist teacher support

RTLB

Resource Teachers: Learning and Behaviour work with schools and kura to strengthen teaching and learning for students with additional learning and behaviour needs.

A useful pathway

  1. Notice patterns and barriers, not labels.
  2. Ask the learner what helps.
  3. Check existing information and support plans.
  4. Talk with colleagues responsible for learning support.
  5. Partner with whānau before seeking individual external support.
  6. Trial reasonable classroom adjustments and record their effect.
  7. Escalate to the appropriate school/Ministry pathway where needs remain significant.
