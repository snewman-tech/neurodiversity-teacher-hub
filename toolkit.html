Conversation tools

Student profile

Ask the learner

What helps you learn? What makes learning harder? How do you like instructions? How can I tell you need a break? What should adults avoid doing? What are your strengths and interests?

Observation

Record useful context

What happened before? What was the task or environment? What did the learner communicate or do? What support was tried? What happened next? What seemed to help?

Transition

Plan before the change

Preview the change, identify a trusted adult, provide a written/visual plan, clarify where the student can go if overwhelmed, and rehearse the first step.

Strengths

Build a fuller picture

Notice humour, creativity, memory, fairness, specialist knowledge, persistence, pattern recognition, empathy, honesty, leadership, problem solving and unconventional thinking.

Teacher reflection questions

  • Is the expectation clear and visible?
  • Does the student know how to start?
  • Is sensory load interfering with access?
  • Does this task require more working memory than I realise?
  • Am I interpreting regulation as defiance?
  • Have I asked the student what works?
  • Is the support discreet enough to protect dignity?
  • What conditions are present when this student succeeds?
