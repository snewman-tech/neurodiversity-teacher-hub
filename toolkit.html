Teacher-ready tools

Student voice

One-page profile prompts

What helps you learn? What makes learning harder? How do you prefer instructions? How can I tell you need a break? What should adults avoid doing? What are your strengths and interests?

Observation

Record context, not judgement

What happened before? What was the task and environment? What did the learner communicate? What support was tried? What happened next? What appeared to help?

Transition

Plan the next change

Preview the event, identify the trusted adult, provide a visual/written sequence, clarify a regulation location and rehearse the first step.

Strengths

Build a balanced learner picture

Notice humour, creativity, memory, fairness, persistence, pattern recognition, specialist knowledge, empathy, honesty, leadership and unconventional problem solving.
