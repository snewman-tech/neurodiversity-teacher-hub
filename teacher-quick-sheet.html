Neurodiversity Teacher Quick Sheet

Use this to identify barriers and supports — not to diagnose.

Before correcting behaviour, ask:

  • Is the instruction clear and visible?
  • Is sensory load too high?
  • Does the learner know how to start?
  • Is working-memory demand high?
  • Is this regulation, overload or confusion?

Try tomorrow:

  • Written steps
  • One small first step
  • Visual timer
  • Transition warning
  • Quiet or movement option
  • Private check-in

Ask the student:

What helps you learn? What makes things harder? How can I tell you need a break? What should I do when you are overwhelmed? What are your strengths and interests?
