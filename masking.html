Students stereotypes can miss

Gender influences expectations and referral patterns, but there is no single “girl presentation” or “boy presentation”. Look beyond stereotypes and notice the effort required to cope.

Quiet presentation

Look for internal effort

  • Daydreaming or appearing “away”
  • Perfectionism and fear of mistakes
  • Slow starts despite strong ability
  • Exhaustion after social or sensory demands
  • Copying peers to work out what to do
  • Strong achievement hiding organisation difficulties
Visible presentation

Do not reduce behaviour to character

  • Movement or impulsivity may support regulation
  • Distress can be a sign of overload
  • Avoid “attention seeking” as an explanation without investigating need
  • Teach replacement skills and change barriers
  • Notice strengths as deliberately as difficulties
Masking has a cost. A student who looks settled may be suppressing natural communication or regulation strategies. Check how sustainable the school day feels for them.
